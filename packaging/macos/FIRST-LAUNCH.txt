kenward.app is not signed by Apple, and it is not notarised.

That is a deliberate choice, not an oversight, and it has one consequence you will meet
immediately: double-clicking the app the first time will fail with a message saying it
is damaged, or that Apple could not check it for malicious software. The app is not
damaged. macOS says that about every unsigned application.

To open it the first time:

  1. Drag kenward.app into Applications.
  2. Open Applications in Finder.
  3. Right-click (or Control-click) kenward, and choose Open.
  4. Click Open in the dialog that appears.

macOS remembers the decision. Every launch after that is an ordinary double-click, and
the login item, if you turn it on, works normally.

On macOS 15 and later the right-click route may be refused as well. If it is, open
System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, and click "Open Anyway"
next to the message about kenward; you will be asked once and never again.

A menu bar icon appears when it starts. There is no window: kenward-desktop supervises
the kenward daemon and opens the dashboard in your own browser.
