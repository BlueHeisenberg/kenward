Add somebody

This creates their private lore space, writes them into the configuration, and mints their claim code — the three things that used to be three commands, and that used to end in a member declared against a space that did not exist.

Their memory

For somebody in the household who does not need a private memory — a teenager, a grandparent, a lodger. kenward answers them in the group and in a chat of their own, out of the household's shared memory alone: everything it remembers from them goes there, where everyone can read it, and it shows them the words and waits for a yes first. They get no private space, no assistant of their own and, in isolated mode, no container of their own. The tiers below do not apply to them; their conversations use the household's chain.

Where their private conversations may go

There is no default. This chain is that person's privacy policy, and nothing here will choose it for them. A chain naming only machines in the house means kenward refuses a turn rather than reaching a provider.
